Why this rating

    Screening aid for exploration and education, not a certified safety analysis. Thresholds are simplified from published cabin and exposure limits (NASA exploration atmosphere study range of 30% O₂ at 10.2 psi; NIOSH CO limits) and are not NASA PSI measurements. Partial gravity is not yet covered by PSI combustion data; microgravity and 1 g results are used as bounds.

    Applications on Earth

    Fine water mist

    Fine-water-mist extinguishers developed for use on the ISS have practical applications here on Earth.

    Smoke detection

    NASA technology has improved terrestrial smoke detection. SAME and DAFT measure smoke and dust aerosols, the data basis for better detectors.

    Firefighter gear

    NASA technology has led to improvements in firefighter protective clothing and air tanks.
