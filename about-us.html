About Us

We are VELTRIXA, a student team building a space intelligence platform that turns NASA’s data into meaningful insights and accessible exploration tools.

Our Mission

To transform NASA's complex microgravity combustion data into an intuitive, AI-driven platform that empowers engineers to design safer spacecraft for the Artemis generation.

Our Vision

A future where the hazards of deep-space exploration are predictable, manageable, and universally understood through accessible open-source intelligence.
